About Us

Hult Prize at Techno India University is a student-led initiative that empowers students to design entrepreneurial solutions for global problems aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

We host Info Sessions, Idea Sprints, Mentorship Clinics and the official Hult Prize On Campus program, helping TIU students build teams, validate ideas, and pitch with confidence.
