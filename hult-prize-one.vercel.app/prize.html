Join the Hult Prize Core Team at Techno India University

Be part of the student leadership team that designs, executes, and scales the Hult Prize OnCampus program at TIU.

Core Team Selection Timeline

The process is designed to be transparent, fair, and skill-focused.

1

Registration

Fill out the application form with honest, detailed responses.

2

Shortlisting

Applications are reviewed based on clarity, intent, and fit.

3

Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a virtual or in-person interaction.

4

Results

Final core team announcement and onboarding.

Core Team Roles

From events and outreach to content and partnerships, there’s a place for every skill set.

1. Social Media & Marketing

Promotes the program through social media, branding, and PR.

2. Sponsorship & Events

Works with brands, startups, and NGOs for sponsorships, manages judges and event execution.

3. Community engagement

Lead outreach and people management to guarantee high-quality event attendance and robust community engagement.

4. Tech

Manages website and technical infrastructure.

5. Editing & Design

Edit and design creatives, posters, decks, brand assets to maintain visual brand identity.

6. Orator

Charismatic Orator needed to be the club's face at live events, delivering high-impact, engaging announcements.

7. Photography & Videography

Manages event photography, videography, recording and shoot reels or promotional content.
